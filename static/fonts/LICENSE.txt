Vendored typefaces for Täppa
============================

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The OFL permits bundling and redistribution with an application. The only
conditions that bind us here: the fonts are not sold on their own, this notice
travels with them, and any modified copy is not shipped under the original name.
The files below are unmodified except for the subsetting Google Fonts applies
when it serves them.

Only the latin and latin-ext subsets are vendored. The interface is Swedish and
English, so cyrillic, greek and vietnamese are deliberately left out.
